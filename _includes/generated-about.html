Fabio Giglietto is Full Professor of Internet Studies at the Department of Communication Sciences, Humanities and International Studies at the University of Urbino Carlo Bo. His research sits at the intersection of computational social science, the analysis of digital platforms, and political communication, with a particular focus on information disorders and coordinated behaviour on social media. His teaching has included courses on Generative AI and Media and Digital Social Network Analysis.

In 2017 he founded the Mapping Italian News Research Program (MINE) at the University of Urbino, which he coordinates as the institutional umbrella for his work on Italian media coverage and the impact of social media on elections, public opinion and health information. Within MINE he co-developed CooRnet, an open-source R package that introduced the Coordinated Link Sharing Behavior (CLSB) detection method, applied internationally by researchers, journalists and fact-checkers. Operational development of CooRnet ended after the shutdown of Meta's CrowdTangle in August 2024, though the CLSB methodology continues to inform independent downstream tools such as CooRTweet, developed by other researchers. His EU-funded work included serving as WP4 Leader of the Horizon Europe project vera.ai, which developed AI-based tools for countering disinformation, and as a partner on PROMPT, dedicated to monitoring disinformation narratives in Europe; both projects have concluded.

His 2025 working paper on Meta's political content reduction policy and the Facebook visibility of Italian parliamentarians was cited in a written question to the European Commission at the European Parliament and drew national media coverage in Italy, including Il Sole 24 Ore, Open, Il Resto del Carlino and Lumsanews.

Beyond research, Giglietto has served the European Commission as an independent expert: he was a member of a Joint Research Centre expert group on systemic risks of very large online platforms, contributing to the European Centre for Algorithmic Transparency under Article 40(12) of the Digital Services Act, and was appointed evaluator and rapporteur for the European Education and Culture Executive Agency. He is a founding partner of Digit Srl, a benefit-corporation spin-off of the University of Urbino that builds digital platforms for sustainability, civic participation, social innovation and scientific dissemination. He is a member of the International Communication Association, the Association of Internet Researchers and the Italian Association for Political Communication, and has served on the board of Research Committee 51 on Sociocybernetics of the International Sociological Association.

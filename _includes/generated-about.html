Fabio Giglietto is Full Professor of Internet Studies at the Università di Urbino Carlo Bo, where he teaches Generative AI and Media and Digital Social Network Analysis. Situated at the intersection of computational social science, digital platform analysis, and political communication, his research focuses on information disorders and coordinated behavior on social media. His recent empirical work investigates the shifting dynamics of platform governance, including how algorithmic policies shape political visibility, the evolution of visual persuasion driven by generative AI, and the increasingly restricted landscape of academic data access in the post-API era. This research has attracted notable public and media attention, covered by major national press outlets such as Il Sole 24 ORE.

He is the founder and coordinator of the Mapping Italian News Research Program (MINE), established in 2017 at the University of Urbino. Serving as an institutional umbrella for his ongoing research line, MINE has hosted a succession of externally funded sub-projects investigating the impact of social media on elections, public opinion, and health information. Although these competitive grants have concluded, leaving the program currently active without external funding, Giglietto recently completed significant European roles under this umbrella. These include serving as the Work Package 4 Leader for the Horizon Europe project vera.ai, which concluded in 2025, and as a partner on the PROMPT initiative, which concluded in 2026.

As part of his methodological contributions to computational social science, Giglietto co-developed CooRnet, an open-source R package that introduced the Coordinated Link Sharing Behavior (CLSB) detection method. Widely adopted by international researchers and investigative journalists, the tool was officially discontinued at the operational level following the shutdown of Meta's CrowdTangle API on August 14, 2024. However, the foundational CLSB methodology lives on in CooRTweet, a downstream, platform-agnostic R package independently developed and maintained by other researchers to detect coordinated behavior across modern social media interfaces.

Beyond his academic and software endeavors, Giglietto is a founding partner (socio fondatore) of Digit Srl, a benefit-corporation spin-off of the University of Urbino Carlo Bo. Digit Srl designs and develops digital platforms dedicated to environmental sustainability, civic participation, social innovation, and scientific dissemination. He is also active in several international scientific communities, holding memberships in the International Communication Association (ICA), the Association of Internet Researchers (AoIR), and the Italian Association for Political Communication, and he has previously served on the board of the International Sociological Association's Research Committee 51 on Sociocybernetics.
